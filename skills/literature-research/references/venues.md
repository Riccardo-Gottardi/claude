# Top STEM venues (anchors)

These are anchors for what a strong venue looks like, not an exhaustive or official ranking. Rankings change: for a venue not listed here, check its SJR quartile (scimagojr.com) or CORE rank (portal.core.edu.au) when you can, and tell the user when you could not. Being on this list does not make a paper good; being absent does not make it bad.

## General science
Nature, Science, PNAS, Nature Communications, Science Advances, and the Nature-family journals for specific fields.

## Machine learning and AI
- Conferences: NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AISTATS, UAI, COLT, AAAI, IJCAI.
- Journals: JMLR, IEEE TPAMI, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, TMLR.

## Computer vision and image processing
- Conferences: CVPR, ICCV, ECCV.
- Journals: IEEE TPAMI, IJCV, IEEE Transactions on Image Processing.

## NLP
ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, TACL.

## Remote sensing and hyperspectral imaging
- Journals: IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (TGRS), ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, Remote Sensing of Environment, IEEE JSTARS.
- Shorter or lower-weight: IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (letters, short), IGARSS (conference), Remote Sensing (MDPI, open access and very high volume: judge each paper on its own merit and corroborate).

## Signal processing
IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing, IEEE Signal Processing Magazine, SIAM Journal on Imaging Sciences.

## Mathematics, statistics and probability
Annals of Statistics, Annals of Probability, JASA, JRSS Series B, Bernoulli, SIAM Review, SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics, Annals of Applied Probability.

## Physics, chemistry, engineering
Physical Review Letters and the Physical Review family, Reviews of Modern Physics, JACS, Proceedings of the IEEE.

## Review and survey venues (good entry points in any field)
ACM Computing Surveys, IEEE Signal Processing Magazine, Proceedings of the IEEE, Foundations and Trends (in Machine Learning, Signal Processing, etc.), Annual Reviews series, Reviews of Modern Physics, SIAM Review.
